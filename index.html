Regis­tration

A Video.js v10 skin built like a two-colour print job: flat ink, a misregistered pink and blue progress bar, and a crosshair thumb. Solid paper panels keep every control readable over any footage.

Play Pause / Unmute Mute Full Exit Close
ConceptPrint, not glass. Every surface is a flat riso ink on cream paper with a hard offset shadow, so the skin looks the same over any footage.
DetailsThe seek fill is two inks printed slightly off-register. The thumb is a registration mark. Buttons press flat, like a stamp.
Access48px targets, a thick focus ring, text labels instead of icons, reduced-motion support, Space/K, M, F and arrow-key shortcuts.
